ABOUT PROJECT

About InterviewMate AI

What is InterviewMate AI?

InterviewMate AI is a web-based mock interview platform designed for students and freshers.

It allows users to select a job role, interview type and difficulty level and practice interview questions.

Users can answer questions by typing or speaking. The system provides answer feedback and communication feedback for spoken answers.

Project Objectives

  • Provide an online mock interview platform.
  • Help students practice interview questions.
  • Analyze user answers.
  • Provide scores and feedback.
  • Identify strengths and weak areas.
  • Improve interview confidence.
